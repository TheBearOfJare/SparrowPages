Privacy Policy

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Sparrow (Application ID: com.thebearofjare.sparrow, "we", "our", or "the app"), available on Android smartphones and Wear OS smartwatches, handles user information. Sparrow provides lightweight, directional compass navigation pointing directly toward your chosen destination.

We believe privacy is a fundamental right. Sparrow is intentionally designed to be privacy-preserving: all distance calculations, compass azimuth processing, and GPS tracking occur strictly on your device. No user location data is ever collected, transmitted to remote servers, or shared with third parties.

✓ On-Device Only 100% local calculation

Information Collection & Use

Sparrow accesses only the minimal permissions and sensor data required to deliver core navigation functions:

  • Location Data (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION): Required in the foreground to measure your current coordinates and calculate real-time distance and magnetic bearing toward your destination. Location data is processed ephemerally in device RAM and is never logged to persistent tracking databases or transmitted off-device.
  • Internet Access (INTERNET): Used solely when you search for a place or street address inside the app (via Android's native Geocoder service) or when resolving a shortened Google Maps share URL (maps.app.goo.gl). Internet access is never used to transmit telemetry, user profiles, or coordinates.
  • Hardware Sensors: Sparrow accesses device orientation sensors (accelerometer and magnetometer via Android's SensorManager) strictly in real-time to align the compass dial with magnetic north. Sensor readings are never stored or exported.
  • Local Destination History: Recent destination coordinates and names are saved strictly within private, sandboxed on-device storage. You can view, re-activate, or permanently clear this history at any time with a single tap.

Phone & Smartwatch Synchronization

Destinations, history, and distance unit settings synchronize between your paired Android phone and Wear OS smartwatch using the Google Play Services Wearable API (DataClient and MessageClient).

This communication is entirely direct and peer-to-peer across your personal Bluetooth or local Wi-Fi pairing. No external third-party servers, cloud sync relays, or intermediary data collectors are involved.

Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information or location data with any third party.

Sparrow includes no third-party advertising SDKs, no behavioral tracking frameworks, and no cloud analytics platforms. There are zero remote servers collecting telemetry from your phone or watch.

Data Retention & Deletion

Sparrow requires no user registration, account creation, or logins. We retain zero user data on remote servers or cloud databases.

Because all processing is ephemeral and local destination history is kept exclusively on your physical device, there is no server-stored personal data to delete. You can erase all saved destinations inside the app's History screen, revoke permissions in system settings, or uninstall the app to purge all local sandbox storage.

Children's Privacy

Sparrow is a general utility app that does not target children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so we can investigate and address it promptly.

Security

Because Sparrow operates entirely within the isolated application sandbox of Android and Wear OS without uploading telemetry to the cloud, your location data and destinations remain confined to your personal hardware.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect app improvements or regulatory updates. Any revisions will be published on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
