Why Sparrow requires Location & Internet on Android & Wear OS

Location Permissions (Foreground Only)

Sparrow (com.thebearofjare.sparrow) is a directional compass navigation app. Its primary function is pointing a real-time vector arrow straight toward your destination with live distance and heading calculations.

To compute your magnetic bearing and remaining distance relative to your destination, Sparrow requests:

  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION: Accesses precise GPS hardware sensors on your phone or smartwatch to orient the directional needle with maximum accuracy.
  • ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION: Provides approximate position data when GPS lock is establishing or battery-saver location modes are active.
✓ In-Use Only No background tracking

Internet Permission (Geocoding Only)

Sparrow includes the INTERNET permission exclusively for two user-initiated actions:

  • In-App Place Search: Converting place names (e.g., "Eiffel Tower") or street addresses into destination coordinates via Android's native Geocoder service.
  • Resolving Shared Map Links: Unwrapping shortened URLs (such as maps.app.goo.gl) when shared from Google Maps so coordinates can be extracted.

Important: Internet access is NEVER used to transmit user location coordinates, upload analytics, or communicate with external tracking servers.

Wear OS Smartwatch Operation

Sparrow is designed for seamless wearable navigation across both companion and standalone modes:

  • Companion Mode (Paired Phone): Destination coordinates and unit preferences synchronize directly between your phone and watch via the Google Play Wearable API (local Bluetooth/Wi-Fi).
  • Standalone Mode: When away from your phone, the Wear OS watch app uses its own built-in GPS sensor to calculate distance and bearing independently.

Data Protection Guarantees

  • Foreground Only: Location data is accessed strictly while Sparrow is open and visible on your screen. The moment the app is closed, minimized, or your screen turns off, all location updates cease.
  • Zero Remote Transmission: Your coordinates are never uploaded to any remote server, cloud database, or external service.
  • Ephemeral In-Memory Math: Coordinates are computed in volatile RAM to orient the compass dial and calculate distance in real-time. No location telemetry logs or route histories are recorded.

Managing Permissions

You can review, grant, or revoke location permissions at any time through system settings:

  1. Open your device Settings (Phone or Watch).
  2. Tap Apps → Sparrow.
  3. Tap Permissions → Location.
  4. Choose "Allow only while using the app" or "Don't allow".

Note: If location permission is revoked, the compass needle cannot calculate the bearing toward your destination.
