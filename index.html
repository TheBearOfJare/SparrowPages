Overview & Vision

Inspired by a certain silly pirate captain's compass, Sparrow provides crows-flight point-A to point-B navigation. Sparrow is designed to give you glancable navigation guidence without the battery drain and overhead of a full maps app, and is great for situations where a maps app simply isn't appropriate.

Designed for both your phone and Wear OS smartwatch, Sparrow is ultra-lightweight, battery-efficient, and privacy-first.

Core Features & Capabilities

One-Tap Google Maps Integration Share dropped pins, business locations, or directions links from Google Maps via Android's native Share menu. Sparrow parses coordinates or shortened links and syncs the destination to your watch immediately.
In-App Geocoder Place Search Search for destinations directly inside the app using place names (e.g., "Eiffel Tower") or full street addresses via Android's native Geocoder.
Dynamic 360° Rotating Compass Dial Smooth 360° rotating dial featuring cardinal points (N, E, S, W) and a high-contrast bearing needle pointing directly to your active destination.
Full Wear OS Support (Companion & Standalone) Bidirectional synchronization keeps destinations synced between phone and watch. The watch app also functions completely standalone using internal GPS when disconnected.
Watch Face Complications Add a mini directional compass arrow (Graphic Complication) or live distance text (Text Complication) directly to your favorite Wear OS watch face.
Destination History & Flexible Units Save recent destinations locally for quick re-selection. Seamlessly toggle between Metric (meters / kilometers) and Imperial (feet / miles) distance units.

Practical Use Cases

Hiking & Trail Walking Keep continuously oriented toward your campsite, summit, or vehicle in off-grid wilderness without needing cell reception or battery-draining map renders.
Geocaching & Off-Road Exploring Walk directly toward destination coordinates across open fields, woodlands, or desert trails.
City Sightseeing Set your hotel or a historic landmark, slip your phone into your pocket, and glance at your smartwatch compass arrow while wandering freely.
Parking Spot Locator Save your parked vehicle location before leaving, then follow the compass needle straight back when returning.

How to Use Sparrow

  1. Share from Google Maps: In Google Maps, tap any place or pin → Share → select Sparrow. The heading sets automatically and syncs to your watch!
  2. Search in App: Open Sparrow, type a destination name or address into the search bar, and tap Find & Set Destination.
  3. Select from History: Tap the History icon in the top app bar to quickly reactivate past destinations.
  4. Toggle Units: Tap the Settings icon to switch between Metric and Imperial distance measurements.
